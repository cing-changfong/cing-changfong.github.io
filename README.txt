# Md Forhad Aziz — Personal Photo Website

This is a static website. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview it.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `YOUR-DOMAIN.example` in `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` with your real domain.
2. Upload the whole folder to your hosting provider.
3. Add the website to Google Search Console and submit the sitemap.
4. Keep the image filenames and descriptive alt text unless you have a good reason to change them.

## SEO note
The site uses your name, Chittagong/Chattogram, and natural photography-related phrases. It does not guarantee a particular Google ranking or that only your photos will appear for a search.

## Photo note
The futuristic 2050 images are presented as creative concepts rather than documentary photographs.
